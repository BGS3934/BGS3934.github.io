My VR city project

A greener way forward

Explore a small city designed to reduce pollution, use clean energy and make room for nature.

01   The sustainability issue

Cities are growing and need cleaner ways to use energy and travel. Without change, pollution and temperatures rise, nature is damaged and people can become less healthy.

02   Solutions around the city

  • Solar panels and wind turbines make clean energy.
  • Electric buses move people with less air pollution.
  • Recycling centres keep useful materials out of landfill.
  • Parks add green space and help improve air quality.

03   Who benefits?

Citizens, local businesses, animals, the environment and future generations all benefit from cleaner air, less waste, better health and more green spaces.

04   Planning and design

I brainstormed the layout and made the city smaller to improve performance. I chose renewable energy and green spaces because they support sustainability.

05   Development progress

I planned the layout, added buildings and sustainable features, then tested the world. I adjusted misplaced objects and reduced object numbers when the world became slow.

Skills improved: coding, problem solving, creativity and VR design.

06   Code snippets

if (energy < 50) { warning = true; } shows a warning when energy is low.

score = score + 10; adds points to the score.

07   Project constraints

Limited time, coding skills, software and device performance meant I removed advanced systems and very detailed buildings.

08   Features for next time

Smart homes, a weather system, interactive buildings and electric trains would make the city more sustainable and realistic. I could not add them because of time and difficulty.

09   Layout and visual design

Clear headings, easy-to-read sections, project screenshots and sustainability colours keep the design organised.

10   Accuracy and clarity

The writing uses my own words, explains technical terms, checks spelling and keeps ideas neat and organised.

Conclusion

My VR city uses clean energy, green spaces and eco-friendly transport to reduce pollution and help create a healthier, more sustainable future for people, nature and Earth.
